07 / BEFORE WE BEGIN

Good questions
deserve real answers.

No vague reassurances. Here's what you should know about our approach.

Do you guarantee admission? +

No. Each university or institute determines its own eligibility, selection and admissions decisions. We help you understand the process and prepare.

Will I see the real college and know the fees? +

We'll discuss verifiable campus information, published costs and available visit arrangements. A physical campus visit is subject to institutional access and feasibility. We don't present unrelated images or claims as verified facts.

Are you officially partnered with every college? +

No. We offer independent admission guidance. Any official appointment or institutional partnership would require written authorisation and will never be implied without it.

Can parents be included in the consultation? +

Of course. Education is a deeply personal family decision. We welcome informed family conversations.

How do we start a private consultation? +

Fill in the form below, select Gagandeep or Abhishek, and send your enquiry through WhatsApp or email. We'll have the course and key details you entered to start a relevant conversation.
